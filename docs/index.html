When should a 0.5B model search instead of answering from memory?

We trained Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct with multi turn GRPO to answer questions with a BM25 search over Wikipedia, and compared four rewards from one shared starting policy. Every trajectory on this page is a recorded greedy run from our evaluation, on the real test questions. Nothing is generated in your browser.

Soft-SAAS does not beat SAASWeighting the penalty by p(q) changed nothing measurable. The success criterion we set in the proposal fails on all three multi hop sets.
Boundary penalties remove redundant searchesSAAS and Soft-SAAS cut 1.5 to 1.8 searches per question against plain GRPO, with no loss of accuracy.
A fixed penalty kills searchAt every λ we tried, search went to zero. GRPO's group normalisation cancels λ whenever all eight answers in a group are wrong.
No policy learned when to searchTrained policies search as often on questions they can answer closed book as on the rest.
One search is the frontierNo trained agent significantly beats a single search with the question as the query on four of the five test sets.

Method in brief

Environment

The policy writes <think>, <search> and <answer> blocks. Each query returns the top 3 BM25 passages from 21M Wikipedia passages (DPR split), at most 4 searches per question. Retrieved text is masked from the loss.

Training

TRL's GRPOTrainer with our own multi turn rollout and colocated vLLM, 8 questions × 8 rollouts per step. A 200 step start policy (plain GRPO with forced exploration), then 100 steps per reward, all branched from the same checkpoint.

Rewards

plain: F1 fixed: F1 − λ·N (every rollout) SAAS: F1 − α·pen(N), only if F1 = 1 soft: F1 − α·[ p·N + (1−p)·max(0, N − N_min) ] N is the number of searches, p = p(q) the start policy's closed book accuracy, α = 0.05.

Evaluation

HotpotQA (1,000) and NQ (500) in domain; 2WikiMultihopQA (1,000), MuSiQue (500) and PopQA (500) out of domain. F1 against the gold answers; p(q) from 8 closed book samples, with a question counted as known when p(q) ≥ 0.5.
